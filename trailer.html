On the panel

The mirror is traced ray by ray through the 4 mm glass, the Llumar R20 and R35 films and the 28 mm air gap, so the tunnel shifts as you walk round.

Port LEDs in the subs

30 cm in

A ring of WS2812B-2020 strip, 3 LEDs wide (3 × 179 per slot), lines the inside of each 46 × 10 cm slot like a bracelet turned inside out, LEDs facing the middle. The slot is raw birch plywood inside. It shows the panel's dominant colour: .

Panel size

Built from parts you can order: 70 cm = 8 P5 modules of 320 × 160 mm (640 mm, 128 × 128); 80 cm = 16 P6 modules of 192 × 192 mm (768 mm, 128 × 128); 90 cm = 25 WS2812B 16 × 16 plus 21 8 × 8 flexible matrices at 10 mm (880 mm, 88 × 88); 1 m = a Dahua PHGOA10-FC P10 cabinet (1000 mm, 96 × 96). The mirror is the LED area plus 4 cm, the slot 1 cm more, and the trailer grows by the extra slot width. The simulator has more LED choices per size.
